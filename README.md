ADSHIELD EXTRA adds a set of Power Tools on top any adblocker — Worry-Free Safe Surfing, a Privacy Inspector, Dynamic DNR filtering, and a Cookie Consent Clicker — plus its own curated block lists for extra ad and tracker coverage. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

WORRY-FREE SAFE SURFING
One-click protection boost for a chosen session (20/60 min/session) when doing random surfing. It tries to auto-dismiss cookie banners, and — for the duration of the session — turns on extra security protections and a download/third-party-code filter that only allows familiar, low-risk regions. That familiar-region list starts from a sensible default (based on your browser's own language setting) but you can freely edit, save, or restore it yourself in the Filter (block) lists panel.

COOKIE CONSENT CLICKER
Automate your cookie consent on the websites you visit often. Simply launch the Cookie-clicker and click the (accept) button to create a rule for it. Next time you open the website, the cookie prompt will be clicked away automatically.

DYNAMIC DNR FILTER
An easy-to-use DNR rule creator for all third-party content, or only 3P-code (scripts, frames), per domain. With a switch you can easily see what domains are allowed or blocked. The Dynamic DNR filter popup is inspired by uBO Mv2's matrix.

PRIVACY INSPECTOR
Inspired by the work of the JShelter project. See what fingerprinting and tracking techniques a website uses, and block them by selecting that specific mechanism — or simply choose a block level: low-risk, medium, or all.

MANAGE CUSTOM RULES
One place to manage your custom cosmetic, DNR, and scriptlet rules created with the tools above. A smart filter lets you view all the rules for whatever domain you type into the search field.

FILTER (BLOCK) LISTS
Built-in filter lists — Kees1958's curated EU+US ads & tracking list, AdGuard tracking servers, Disconnect, and Peter Lowe's for tracking protection; Easylist adult advertising servers, AdGuard anti-adblock, and Anti-Admiral for annoyance protection — are all enabled by default and can each be switched off individually.

ALLOW (WHITE) LIST
Based on AdGuard's allowlist. Easily copy and paste domains into the allow list to disable filtering on that domain.

PRIVACY & SECURITY 
Includes an option to delete browser history on startup (except passwords), plus strong additional Security & Privacy protections, each with a low risk of breaking websites.
